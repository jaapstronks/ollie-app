# First-Time User Journey: Emma & Milo

A documented Maestro test that simulates the complete first-time user experience from onboarding through initial event logging.

---

## The Persona: Emma Johnson

**Demographics**
- Age: 28 years old
- Location: Amsterdam, Netherlands
- Occupation: UX Designer (works from home)
- Living situation: 2-bedroom apartment with small balcony, no yard

**Personality**
- Organized and detail-oriented (hence wanting an app!)
- Slightly anxious first-time dog owner
- Active on Reddit's r/puppy101 community
- Takes lots of photos

---

## The Puppy: Milo

**Profile**
- Breed: Golden Retriever
- Age: 9 weeks old
- Color: Classic golden
- Came home: 3 days ago

**Personality**
- Playful but tires easily (typical puppy!)
- Loves belly rubs and his stuffed duck toy
- Cries in crate for first 10 minutes, then settles
- Food motivated - great for training

---

## Emma's Situation (Why She Downloaded the App)

Emma has wanted a dog her entire life. She researched breeds for 2 years, found a reputable breeder, and finally brought Milo home last Saturday. The first 3 days have been... challenging:

**The Good**
- Milo is healthy and sweet
- He's starting to recognize his name
- He sleeps through the night (mostly)
- Already knows "sit" (food motivation!)

**The Struggles**
- 3 indoor pee accidents yesterday alone
- She keeps forgetting to take him out after meals
- No idea if his sleep schedule is normal
- Feels like she's failing even though everyone says "puppies are hard"

**How She Found Ollie**
- Posted on r/puppy101: "I feel like I'm drowning. Is there an app that actually helps?"
- Someone recommended Ollie: "It tracks patterns and tells you when your puppy will probably need to pee"
- She downloaded it immediately

**What She Hopes For**
1. Reminders so she stops having "I forgot to take him out!" moments
2. Patterns that help her predict Milo's needs
3. Some validation that she's doing okay (seeing progress over time)
4. Less mental load - let the app remember so she doesn't have to

### Emma's Journey Through the App

#### Phase 1: Onboarding (7:05-7:10am)

Emma opens the app while Milo chews on her slipper. The welcome screen catches her attention:

> "Already in the deep end?"

"Yes!" she thinks. "That's exactly how I feel."

She taps **Get Started** and enters Milo's information:
- Name: "Milo" (named after her late grandfather's dog)
- Breed: Golden Retriever (she scrolls down and finds it quickly)
- Birth date: ~9 weeks ago
- Status: "Yes, Milo is home" (with feeling!)
- Home date: A few days ago

She skips the photo step - she has 500 photos of Milo but wants to get to the tracking features first.

The notification permission screen appears and she immediately enables it. Reminders are the #1 reason she downloaded the app.

**Emma's verdict on onboarding:** "Fast and painless. Didn't ask for anything weird. I like that it didn't make me create an account or give my email."

#### Phase 2: First Log - Morning Pee Success (7:15-7:20am)

Fresh from taking Milo outside (he peed immediately, the good boy), Emma wants to log it while it's fresh in her mind.

She notices the floating button in the corner and taps it. A menu appears with emoji icons. She taps the pee drop icon.

A simple sheet slides up asking "Inside or Outside?" She taps **Outside**, then **Done**.

That's it. Two taps.

**Emma's thoughts:** "Wait, that's all? I was expecting some complicated form. This might actually work."

#### Phase 3: Logging Breakfast (7:30-7:35am)

Milo demolishes his breakfast in approximately 45 seconds. Emma logs the meal with the same two-tap flow.

She's starting to feel a rhythm.

#### Phase 4: The Inevitable Accident (7:50-7:57am)

Twenty minutes after eating, Milo squats on the living room rug.

"MILO NO!" Emma scoops him up mid-stream and rushes to the balcony. He finishes outside.

She logs both events:
1. Inside pee (the accident)
2. Outside pee (the recovery)

**Emma's internal monologue:** "I almost didn't want to log the accident. But accurate data = accurate predictions, right? No point lying to an app. At least now there's a record that 20 minutes post-meal is a danger zone."

#### Phase 5: Sweet Relief - Nap Time (8:15am)

Milo is getting drowsy. Emma puts him in his crate with a frozen stuffed Kong. He whines for about 30 seconds, licks the Kong twice, and passes out.

She logs the nap start and finally sits down with her coffee.

#### Phase 6: Exploring (8:17am)

While Milo sleeps, Emma pokes around the app:

- **Today tab**: Shows her morning timeline. She can see all 5 events she logged. Satisfying!
- **Insights tab**: Mostly empty since she just started, but she can see it'll show potty gaps and patterns
- **Train tab**: Lists puppy skills to work on. She makes a mental note to come back here
- **Walks tab**: For tracking walks and routes. Not relevant yet since Milo can't go on real walks until his vaccines are complete

**Emma's verdict after exploring:** "Clean interface, doesn't feel overwhelming. I can see this being part of my daily routine."

## Running the Test

```bash
# Run the complete journey
maestro test .maestro/scenarios/first-time-user-journey.yaml

# Run with visible browser (slower but good for demos)
maestro test .maestro/scenarios/first-time-user-journey.yaml --debug-output

# Run and generate HTML report
maestro test .maestro/scenarios/first-time-user-journey.yaml --format html
```

**Prerequisites:**
- iOS Simulator running with iPhone 15 Pro or similar
- App built and installed on simulator
- Maestro CLI installed (`brew install maestro`)

## Notes for Product Team

This test documents several UX observations:

**What Works Well**
- Two-tap event logging is fast
- Onboarding doesn't require account creation
- Permission requests explain benefits clearly

**Potential Improvements**
- After first outdoor pee, could show celebration/encouragement
- Could suggest "time since last potty" on main screen
- Post-meal might benefit from automatic reminder (20-30 min later)
