# Recall Training — The Magic Word

The most important command your dog will ever learn. A reliable recall can save your dog's life.

---

## Why a Special Word (Not Their Name)

- You use the dog's name way too often and casually — it loses all value.
- "Come" / "here" also gets devalued through overuse without reward.
- The magic word should feel like **the ice cream truck** to the dog — irresistible excitement every single time.

---

## Choosing Your Magic Word

- Pick something that makes you happy: "happy," "party," "fiesta," "chickie," etc.
- It must be a word you won't use in daily life by accident.
- It must be something you're not embarrassed to shout in public.
- Avoid words that overlap with pet names you already use casually.
- Consider a triple-word cue for activities like swimming: "swim, swim, swim" — not single use, because a single mention in conversation will be misinterpreted.

---

## Training the Magic Word

### Phase 1: Build the association

- Pair the word with **the best food**: boiled chicken breast, young cheese (low salt), small pieces of premium treats, dried sprats.
- Crouch down, wave your arms (this becomes a visual hand signal for distance/deaf dogs).
- Say the word → dog comes → click → give the high-value treat.

### Phase 2: Add a sit

- Once the dog comes reliably, add a sit after they arrive.
- One treat for coming, one treat for sitting.
- This prevents the "drive-by" where the dog runs past you.

### Phase 3: Standing position

- Practice while standing instead of crouching.
- The dog learns to come to you regardless of your posture.

### Phase 4: Add distractions

- Put a snuffle mat on the ground, let the dog engage with it.
- Call the magic word.
- Click when they turn around → reward immediately.
- Gradually increase distraction difficulty.

---

## The Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Never** use this word without delivering the amazing reward.
2. Everyone in the household must use it — and deliver the goods.
3. Bring high-value treats to every practice session and every walk.
4. This is the easiest, most important, and most fun exercise.
5. The recall command gets the **best** treats — reserved exclusively for this.

---

## Why This Matters

A dog that reliably comes when called:
- Can be trusted off-leash
- Can be called away from dangerous situations
- Can be retrieved before conflicts with other dogs
- Can enjoy more freedom safely

This is worth investing in properly. Don't cut corners on the reward quality.
