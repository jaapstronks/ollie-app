Privacy Policy

Otis - Puppy Logbook
Last updated: March 2025

The short version: Your puppy's data stays yours. We don't sell data, show ads, or share your information with third parties for marketing. Analytics are minimal and hosted in the EU.

What Data We Collect

Otis collects the following data to provide the app's features:

  • Puppy profile: Name, breed, birth date, home date, and size category
  • Events: Logged activities (meals, potty breaks, sleep, training, etc.) with timestamps and optional notes
  • Photos & videos: Media you choose to attach to events
  • Location data: GPS location when you use places, walks, or navigation features (only when you grant permission)
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous app usage data (screens viewed, features used) to improve the app
  • Crash reports: Technical information when the app crashes, to help us fix bugs

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the app's core functionality (tracking, predictions, insights)
  • To sync your data across your devices via iCloud (if enabled)
  • To share data with family members you explicitly invite
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns
  • To fix crashes and technical issues

Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: All puppy data is stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch
  • iCloud: If you enable sync, data is stored in your personal iCloud account (managed by Apple)
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data is processed on servers in the European Union
  • Crash reports: Processed by Sentry (see their privacy policy)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Your data may be shared only in these cases:

  • Family sharing: With other users you explicitly invite to share your puppy's profile
  • Service providers: With technical services that help run the app (Apple/iCloud for sync, Sentry for crash reports) — these providers are contractually obligated to protect your data
  • Legal requirements: If required by law

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored in the app
  • Export: Export your data at any time
  • Delete: Delete your data by removing the app or through the app's settings
  • Withdraw consent: Disable location access, iCloud sync, or analytics at any time in Settings

For EU residents: You have additional rights under GDPR including data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Data Retention

Your puppy data is retained as long as you use the app. If you delete the app, local data is removed. iCloud data can be managed through your iCloud settings. Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 24 months.

Children's Privacy

Otis is designed to track puppies, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app may be used by families, and parents/guardians are responsible for their children's use of the app.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and at rest. iCloud data is protected by Apple's security infrastructure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the "Last updated" date above.
